# Evvy-BV-UTI
Analysis of participants with BV and/or UTI using vaginal samples from Evvy participants based on the Nature Communications publication "Large-scale metagenomic characterization of the vaginal microbiome and uropathogens in women with recent bacterial vaginosis and urinary tract infections" https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-026-78217-1 

Also previously available as a pre-print "Vaginal Microbiome in Women Recently Experiencing BV & UTI" https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.1101/2025.11.27.690987v1.full
